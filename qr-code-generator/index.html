    Reference

    QR code usage tips

    Best print size

    Print at least 2 x 2 cm (0.8 in) for close-up scanning such as receipts or packaging; scale up for distance.

    Scanning distance

    As a rule of thumb, a QR code can be scanned from about 10 times its width — a 10 cm code scans well from roughly 1 meter away.

    Indoor vs. outdoor

    Outdoor and storefront codes benefit from higher error correction and matte finishes to reduce glare from direct sunlight.

    Placement

    Place codes at eye level with clear space around them — a cluttered background is one of the most common causes of failed scans.

    Contrast first

    Dark modules on a light background scan far more reliably than the reverse, especially at small sizes.

    Test before you print

    Always scan a printed proof with two or three different phones before ordering a large print run.

    Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this QR code generator really free?

    Yes. Every QR type, customization option, and download format on this page is free to use, with no watermark and no account required.

    Do the QR codes expire?

    No. The codes generated here encode your content directly as static QR codes, so they never expire and aren't tied to our servers.

    Can I add my logo to a QR code?

    Yes. Upload a logo and adjust its size in the customization panel. Use Quartile or High error correction so the code stays reliable with a logo overlay.

    What is the best error correction level for a QR code with a logo?

    Quartile (Q) or High (H), since both let the code stay scannable even when part of the pattern is covered by your logo.

    What file format should I download my QR code in?

    SVG for large-format print like posters and signage, PNG for digital use or a transparent background, and JPG for quick sharing.

    How do I create a WiFi QR code?

    Choose the WiFi type, enter your network name, password, and encryption type. Guests scan to join without typing anything.

    Can I generate a WhatsApp QR code?

    Yes — choose WhatsApp, enter your number and an optional prefilled message, and scanning opens a chat directly.

    What size should I print a QR code?

    A minimum of about 2 x 2 cm for close range, scaling up roughly 1 inch of width for every 10 feet of expected scanning distance.

    Why won't my QR code scan?

    Usually low contrast, an oversized logo, too little quiet zone around the code, or printing it too small for the distance it'll be scanned from.

    Can I make a QR code for a vCard or contact card?

    Yes — choose Contact (vCard) and fill in name, phone, email, and company so people can save your details straight to their contacts.

    Is a rounded or dotted QR code as reliable as a square one?

    Yes, as long as contrast stays high — the three corner finder patterns stay clearly defined automatically, regardless of dot style.

    Can I use these QR codes commercially?

    Yes, with no licensing restrictions — use them on business cards, packaging, menus, storefronts, and marketing materials freely.

    Can I make a QR code for a cloud file like a PDF, Word doc, or ZIP?

    Yes — choose the matching cloud file type and paste the public link from Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, or wherever the file is hosted. The file itself is never uploaded here.

    Can I generate a QR code for Spotify, Netflix, or a game store page?

    Yes — the Entertainment category covers Spotify, Apple Music, Netflix, Steam, and Epic Games links, all encoded the same reliable way as any other URL.

    Is there a QR code type for online courses or classroom links?

    Yes — the Education category includes course pages, classrooms, notes, presentations, and assignments, useful for handouts and syllabi.

    Can I create a QR code linking to a payment page or Google Review?

    Yes — the Business category includes payment links and Google Review links, both handy for receipts, invoices, and follow-up cards.

    Do I need an internet connection to use this generator?

    Only to load the page and its font/icon library the first time. Once loaded, QR generation itself runs locally in your browser with no further network requests.

    Will my saved history sync between devices?

    No — history and favorites are stored in this browser's local storage only, so they stay on this device and aren't sent anywhere or shared across devices.

    What's the difference between a static and dynamic QR code?

    A static QR code encodes your content directly and never expires. A dynamic QR code points to a redirect service you can edit later, which requires a backend — this tool intentionally only creates static codes so nothing can break or get paywalled later.

    Can I edit a QR code after downloading it?

    Not the downloaded image itself, but you can return to the generator, adjust any setting, and export a new file at any time — your inputs stay in the form until you change them.
